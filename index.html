About

I am a Junior Research Fellow at the Development Policy Research Unit (DPRU) in the School of Economics at the University of Cape Town, and an Affiliate Researcher in the Global Researcher Network of the Anker Research Institute.

My research is on South African labour markets: how good jobs are, who is covered by social protection, what workers need to earn to live decently, and how policy shapes these outcomes. I work mainly with household survey and administrative data, including the Quarterly Labour Force Survey, and have contributed to living wage estimates using the Anker methodology.

Most of my work is collaborative and written with policy use in mind, prepared with colleagues at the DPRU and for partners including government departments, the National Minimum Wage Commission, the Agence Française de Développement, the Brookings Institution and the Anker Research Institute. I hold Honours and Master's degrees in Economic Science from the University of the Witwatersrand.
